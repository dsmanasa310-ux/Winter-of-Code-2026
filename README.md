# Know your weather. Know your risks. Be ready.

Explore 18 places across India: seasonal weather, the disasters each is prone to, and exactly what to do before, during and after.

**Please read.** Charts show long-term typical values, rounded, and risk levels are simple learning guides. This app shows no live weather or real warnings. For today’s alerts use the official sources on the Help tab.
